I massively simplified and excluded extra features I didn't need, such as the GPS, to just keep the core functionality. I switched from an MCU microchip to and Xiao ESP32-C6 and deleted the bio-measurement components. I only kept the vibrator, as that was the component that was most important to making this watch be an effective reminder. I added power protection and charging components, and a USB-C port. 

Then, I wired the components together starting with the ESP32-C6 wirings for the main functions, and the adding passives and wiring the power system. I used mostly net labels.

Then, I converted the schematic into a PCB using the Easyeda feature. I strategically placed the screen and main components on opposite sides to make the PCB compact enough for a watch. Then I autorouted.

Then, I exported appropriate files
